YouTube to Note

Companion for the Obsidian plugin

Click the purple icon in the YouTube player, click the toolbar button, or press Ctrl+Shift+Y (⌘+Shift+Y on macOS).

Works on watch pages, Shorts, live streams, embedded players opened as a page, and music.youtube.com. Your current playback position is carried over as a timestamp.

Every video is sent to Obsidian as a plain watch link:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ID&t=1m30s

Uses obsidian://youtube-clipper?url= — no extra plugins needed.

Nothing opened?

  • Obsidian must be running with the YouTube to Note plugin enabled
  • The browser must be allowed to open obsidian:// links — if Chrome asks "Open Obsidian?", tick Always allow
  • The confirmation bubble appears as soon as the link is handed to your operating system; it cannot confirm that Obsidian actually opened

There is no button:

  • Only YouTube pages show one — not the home, search or channel pages, and not players embedded in other websites
  • Navigating inside YouTube (next video, back to home) adds or removes it automatically; reload the tab if it goes missing

Unofficial companion — not affiliated with, or endorsed by, YouTube or Obsidian.
